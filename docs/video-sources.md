# Hero video sources

The home hero video is built from bathroom photographs used with permission.

Two versions are rendered with ffmpeg (Pillow frames, slow Ken Burns zoom and crossfades, seamless 18-second loop, no audio):

- Desktop, 1280x720: three shower panels side by side, each crossfading between two showers on a staggered timer. `hero-desktop.mp4` (H.264, about 2.9 MB), `hero-desktop.webm` (VP9, about 1.6 MB), `hero-desktop-poster.webp`.
- Mobile, 720x1280: one full-screen shower at a time, six shots. `hero-mobile.mp4` (about 1.9 MB), `hero-mobile.webm` (about 1.5 MB), `hero-mobile-poster.webp`.

Each visitor downloads only one version. Render scripts: `scripts/hero-video/`.

Shots used (local files):

- Walk-in shower with window and vanity — public/images/hero-walkin.webp
- Glass-enclosed custom shower with seat — public/images/shower-arch.webp
- Rain-head walk-in shower with niche — public/images/hero-rain.webp
- Barn-door shower with corner seat — public/images/shower-barn.webp
- Frameless glass shower, warm tones — public/images/shower-warm.webp
- Tub surround with sliding glass door — public/images/tub-surround.webp
