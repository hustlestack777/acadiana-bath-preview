The surface

Meet your next shower surface.

The Onyx Collection combines mineral-derived alumina trihydrate with a specially formulated resin. The result is a cast bathroom material with a range of colors and a finish designed for regular care with non-abrasive cleaners.

Find the look that feels like home. Choose your finish and talk through the details with Justin before deciding what goes into your bathroom.

63% alumina trihydrate, a mineral-derived ingredient, and 37% specially formulated polyester resin.

It is a cast bathroom material. It is not a slab of quarried natural onyx.

Care

Less fuss on cleaning day.

Onyx says a simple weekly cleaning is often enough to keep its showers looking new. Follow the manufacturer’s care instructions and use non-abrasive cleaners to protect the finish.

Manufacturer product warranty

A manufacturer that stands behind its products.

The Onyx Collection publishes a manufacturer product warranty. Its published statement says that if its product fails, it will provide a new item or refund the cost. Ask us to review the manufacturer’s current terms with you. Installation workmanship and any labor coverage are separate and must be confirmed with Justin.

Questions

Is Onyx natural stone?+

No. The Onyx Collection describes its product as 63% alumina trihydrate, a mineral-derived ingredient, and 37% specially formulated polyester resin. It is not a slab of quarried natural onyx.

Can photos give me an exact quote?+

Photos help start the conversation. Final pricing depends on measurements, selected materials, installation scope and conditions that may not be visible in a photo. Justin confirms the proposal before work begins.

What does a remodel cost?+

Your layout, selections and scope determine the price. Share your project details so we can help you understand your options.

What areas do you cover?+

Planned coverage for Justin to confirm: Lake Charles, Jennings, Crowley, Lafayette, Broussard, Youngsville, New Iberia, Opelousas, Alexandria, Franklin, Morgan City, Thibodaux, Houma and Baton Rouge. Prairieville and Gonzales are not included. This is not an office in each town.
